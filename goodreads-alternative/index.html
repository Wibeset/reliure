Why readers choose Reliure

  • No account, no sign-up. Open the app and add your first book. There is no profile to create and no password to remember.
  • Private by default. Your library, reading sessions, notes and ratings are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We never receive them.
  • Built around reading, not scrolling. No feed and no ads: your current book, your page and your progress.
  • Works offline. Everything works without a connection, except looking up a book online.
  • Tropes, spice and series. Tag each book with its tropes, rate its spice from Mild to Scorching, and keep series together, in order, with the next volume ready to start. See the BookTok tracker.
  • Let the wheel pick your next read. Spin it to choose among your books to read, or among books by the authors you loved and in the genres you read most.
  • Remember what moved you. Photograph a page and select a passage to keep it as a quote, with notes and ratings by theme.
  • Watch the habit grow. Reading sessions, statistics, streaks, a yearly goal, a reading calendar, twelve reading levels and a monthly challenge.
  • Your library stays yours. Back it up to a file, restore it on a new iPhone, or export it to CSV at any time.

When Goodreads or StoryGraph may suit you better

Reliure is deliberately simple. You may prefer Goodreads or StoryGraph if you want to follow friends and read their reviews, use Android or a web browser, or track ebooks and audiobooks: Reliure is designed for print books, tracked by page.

Move from Goodreads in two steps

  1. On the Goodreads website, open My Books › Import and export and choose Export library to download a CSV file.
  2. Import that CSV file into Reliure. Your books come with you, and you can keep reading where you left off.

Changed your mind later? Reliure exports your library in the Goodreads CSV format, which Goodreads and StoryGraph can both import. Coming from StoryGraph instead? See Reliure as a StoryGraph alternative.

The best Goodreads alternatives

We make Reliure, so it comes first. We’ve described every other app as fairly as we can, with what it does best.

1. Reliure

Reliure keeps what Goodreads does for your own reading (shelves, ratings, a yearly goal) and leaves out the account, the feed and the ads. Import your Goodreads CSV, scan the rest, and time your sessions with the reading timer.

Price: free, with no ads, no subscription and no account. iPhone, iOS 18 or later. Download on the App Store.

2. StoryGraph

Mood, pace and theme tags, detailed charts and recommendations, with audiobooks tracked in minutes. Free with an account; StoryGraph Plus is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year. iOS, Android and web. Best for mood-based recommendations and detailed charts. See alternatives to StoryGraph.

3. Hardcover

A modern social tracker with a friends feed, lists, reviews and community-edited book data. Free with an account; the Supporter plan is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year. iOS, Android and web. Best for a Goodreads-style social network with a newer design. See alternatives to Hardcover.

4. Fable

Online book clubs and TV clubs with chapter-by-chapter discussions, a built-in ebook reader and an ebook store. Free with an account; Fable Plus is $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year. Best for reading along with an online book club. See alternatives to Fable.

5. Book Tracker

An indie tracker with no account, a reading timer, an Apple Watch app, and imports from Goodreads, StoryGraph and BookBuddy. Free up to 5 books; Pro is a one-time $11.49. iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac (sold separately). Best for an Apple Watch app and a one-time price. See alternatives to Book Tracker.

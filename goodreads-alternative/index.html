Why readers choose Reliure

  • No account, no sign-up. Open the app and add your first book. There is no profile to create and no password to remember.
  • Private by default. Your library, reading sessions, notes and ratings are stored on your iPhone. We never receive them.
  • Built around reading, not scrolling. No feed and no ads: your current book, your page and your progress.
  • Works offline. Everything works without a connection, except looking up a book online.
  • Remember what moved you. Photograph a page and select a passage to keep it as a quote, with notes and ratings by theme.
  • Watch the habit grow. Reading sessions, statistics, streaks, a yearly goal, a reading calendar, twelve reading levels and a monthly challenge.
  • Your library stays yours. Back it up to a file, restore it on a new iPhone, or export it to CSV at any time.

When Goodreads or StoryGraph may suit you better

Reliure is deliberately simple. You may prefer Goodreads or StoryGraph if you want to follow friends and read their reviews, use Android or a web browser, or track ebooks and audiobooks: Reliure is designed for print books, tracked by page.

Move from Goodreads in two steps

  1. On the Goodreads website, open My Books › Import and export and choose Export library to download a CSV file.
  2. Import that CSV file into Reliure. Your books come with you, and you can keep reading where you left off.

Changed your mind later? Reliure exports your library in the Goodreads CSV format, which Goodreads and StoryGraph can both import.

Reading tracker alternatives

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Reliure is a free reading tracker for iPhone: no account, no ads and no subscription, with your library on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Here is how it compares with the apps readers most often leave.

Download Reliure on the App Store: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.

Compare Reliure with

  • Alternatives to Goodreads: The largest reading community, owned by Amazon: friends, reviews, groups and the yearly Reading Challenge. Free, with an account, and ads on the website. iOS, Android and web.
  • Alternatives to StoryGraph: Mood, pace and theme tags, detailed charts and recommendations, with audiobooks tracked in minutes. Free with an account; StoryGraph Plus is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year. iOS, Android and web.
  • Alternatives to Hardcover: A modern social tracker with a friends feed, lists, reviews and community-edited book data. Free with an account; the Supporter plan is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year. iOS, Android and web.
  • Alternatives to Fable: Online book clubs and TV clubs with chapter-by-chapter discussions, a built-in ebook reader and an ebook store. Free with an account; Fable Plus is $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year.
  • Alternatives to Bookly: A reading timer, reading speed, detailed statistics and readathons, with no account needed. The free version is limited and shows ads; Bookly Pro is $29.99 a year or $79.99 for life. iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android.
  • Alternatives to Basmo: A reading timer on the Lock Screen, AI features to ask questions about a book or recap it, and Kindle highlight sync. Most features need Basmo Premium, a subscription. iOS, Mac and Android.
  • Alternatives to Bookmory: A private reading journal with a timer, statistics by author, publisher and tag, and notes from photos. Free with ads; Premium is $3.49 a month or $30.99 a year. iOS, Mac and Android.
  • Alternatives to BookBuddy: A catalogue for your home library: barcode scanning, loans, tags and exports to CSV, PDF and HTML. Free up to 50 books; Pro is a one-time $9.99. iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Alternatives to Book Tracker: An indie tracker with no account, a reading timer, an Apple Watch app, and imports from Goodreads, StoryGraph and BookBuddy. Free up to 5 books; Pro is a one-time $11.49. iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac (sold separately).
  • Alternatives to Bookshelf: Reading sessions, charts and streaks, plus AI book summaries and recommendations. Free; Bookshelf Gold is $29.99 a year or $79.99 for life, and AI features use paid tokens. iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Based on publicly available information and US App Store prices as of November 2026. All names are trademarks of their respective owners; Reliure is not affiliated with any of them.

What every comparison has in common

  • No account, no sign-up. Open the app and add your first book by scanning its barcode.
  • Private by default. Your library, reading sessions, notes and ratings stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We never receive them.
  • Free for good. No ads, no subscription, no book limit and nothing locked behind a paid plan.
  • Your library stays yours. Import a Goodreads CSV, and export your library in the same format at any time.

Reliure is designed for print books on iPhone. If you need Android, the web, ebooks or audiobooks, each comparison says which app may suit you better.

A StoryGraph alternative, without an account

Last updated: November 20, 2026

StoryGraph is a good reading tracker if you want an online account, recommendations and a community. Reliure takes another path: a free iPhone app with no account and no subscription, where your library stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Why readers choose Reliure

  • No account, no sign-up. Open the app and add your first book by scanning its barcode.
  • Private by default. Your library, reading sessions, notes and ratings stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We never receive them.
  • Tropes, spice and series. Tag each book with its tropes, rate its spice from Mild to Scorching, and keep series together, in order.
  • Reading statistics. Pages read, sessions, streaks, a yearly goal, a reading calendar and your favourite tropes of the year.
  • Quotes from a photo. Photograph a page and select a passage to keep it.
  • Made to share. Your bookcase, a month or your year in reading as an image, for a post or a story.
  • Free for good. No ads, no subscription and nothing locked behind a paid plan.

When StoryGraph may suit you better

You may prefer StoryGraph if you want recommendations based on mood and pace, reading with friends and a community, Android or a web browser, or tracking ebooks and audiobooks: Reliure is designed for print books, tracked by page.

Bring your books to Reliure

  1. If your books are also on Goodreads, export your library from Goodreads as a CSV file and import it into Reliure.
  2. Otherwise, scan the barcodes of the books you’re reading now: each one takes a few seconds.

Reliure exports your library in the Goodreads CSV format, which StoryGraph can import, so you can always go back.

Download Reliure on the App Store: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.

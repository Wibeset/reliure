Last updated: November 20, 2026

Why readers choose Reliure

  • No account, no sign-up. Open the app and add your first book by scanning its barcode.
  • Private by default. Your library, reading sessions, notes and ratings stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We never receive them.
  • Tropes, spice and series. Tag each book with its tropes, rate its spice from Mild to Scorching, and keep series together, in order. See the BookTok tracker.
  • Reading statistics. Pages read, sessions, streaks, a yearly goal, a reading calendar and your favourite tropes of the year.
  • Quotes from a photo. Photograph a page and select a passage to keep it.
  • Made to share. Your bookcase, a month or your year in reading as an image, for a post or a story.
  • Free for good. No ads, no subscription and nothing locked behind a paid plan.

When StoryGraph may suit you better

You may prefer StoryGraph if you want recommendations based on mood and pace, reading with friends and a community, Android or a web browser, or tracking ebooks and audiobooks: Reliure is designed for print books, tracked by page.

Bring your books to Reliure

  1. If your books are also on Goodreads, export your library from Goodreads as a CSV file and import it into Reliure.
  2. Otherwise, scan the barcodes of the books you’re reading now: each one takes a few seconds.

Reliure exports your library in the Goodreads CSV format, which StoryGraph can import, so you can always go back. Leaving Goodreads too? See Reliure as a Goodreads alternative.

The best StoryGraph alternatives

We make Reliure, so it comes first. We’ve described every other app as fairly as we can, with what it does best.

1. Reliure

Reliure gives you reading statistics, a reading timer, tropes and spice for free, with no account and no StoryGraph Plus: your library stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Price: free, with no ads, no subscription and no account. iPhone, iOS 18 or later. Download on the App Store.

2. Hardcover

A modern social tracker with a friends feed, lists, reviews and community-edited book data. Free with an account; the Supporter plan is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year. iOS, Android and web. Best for a Goodreads-style social network with a newer design. See alternatives to Hardcover.

3. Goodreads

The largest reading community, owned by Amazon: friends, reviews, groups and the yearly Reading Challenge. Free, with an account, and ads on the website. iOS, Android and web. Best for following friends and reading other people’s reviews. See alternatives to Goodreads.

4. Bookly

A reading timer, reading speed, detailed statistics and readathons, with no account needed. The free version is limited and shows ads; Bookly Pro is $29.99 a year or $79.99 for life. iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android. Best for a timer and statistics on every Apple device, if you don’t mind a subscription. See alternatives to Bookly.

5. Book Tracker

An indie tracker with no account, a reading timer, an Apple Watch app, and imports from Goodreads, StoryGraph and BookBuddy. Free up to 5 books; Pro is a one-time $11.49. iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac (sold separately). Best for an Apple Watch app and a one-time price. See alternatives to Book Tracker.

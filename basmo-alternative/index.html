Why readers look for a Basmo alternative

Readers often find Basmo expensive for what they use, since many features sit behind Premium, and some report buttons that stop working after updates.

The best Basmo alternatives

We make Reliure, so it comes first. We’ve described every other app as fairly as we can, with what it does best.

1. Reliure

Reliure covers the everyday part of Basmo for free. Start the reading timer and follow it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island; when you stop, a wheel of page numbers is already turned to where you most likely stopped. Photograph a page to keep a quote, add notes, and watch your streaks, reading time and pages per hour grow, with no subscription and no account.

Price: free, with no ads, no subscription and no account. iPhone, iOS 18 or later. Download on the App Store.

2. Bookly

A reading timer, reading speed, detailed statistics and readathons, with no account needed. The free version is limited and shows ads; Bookly Pro is $29.99 a year or $79.99 for life. iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android. Best for a timer and statistics on every Apple device, if you don’t mind a subscription. See alternatives to Bookly.

3. Bookmory

A private reading journal with a timer, statistics by author, publisher and tag, and notes from photos. Free with ads; Premium is $3.49 a month or $30.99 a year. iOS, Mac and Android. Best for tracking print books, ebooks and audiobooks in one journal. See alternatives to Bookmory.

4. Book Tracker

An indie tracker with no account, a reading timer, an Apple Watch app, and imports from Goodreads, StoryGraph and BookBuddy. Free up to 5 books; Pro is a one-time $11.49. iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac (sold separately). Best for an Apple Watch app and a one-time price. See alternatives to Book Tracker.

5. Bookshelf

Reading sessions, charts and streaks, plus AI book summaries and recommendations. Free; Bookshelf Gold is $29.99 a year or $79.99 for life, and AI features use paid tokens. iPhone, iPad and Mac. Best for AI summaries and recommendations next to tracking. See alternatives to Bookshelf.

When Basmo may suit you better

Basmo may suit you better if you want AI features to ask questions about a book, Kindle highlight or Notion sync, Android, or audiobook tracking.

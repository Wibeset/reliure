Why readers look for a Fable alternative

Fable mixes reading tracking with clubs, a feed, an ebook store and TV shows. Readers who just want to log their pages sometimes find it busy, report lag and loading failures, or would rather not have their reading tied to an online account.

The best Fable alternatives

We make Reliure, so it comes first. We’ve described every other app as fairly as we can, with what it does best.

1. Reliure

Reliure keeps what BookTok readers love about Fable and drops the noise. Tag each book with its tropes, rate its spice from Mild to Scorching, keep series in order, and share your bookcase or your year in reading as an image. There is no account, no feed and no store: just your current book, your page and your progress, stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. See the BookTok tracker.

Price: free, with no ads, no subscription and no account. iPhone, iOS 18 or later. Download on the App Store.

2. StoryGraph

Mood, pace and theme tags, detailed charts and recommendations, with audiobooks tracked in minutes. Free with an account; StoryGraph Plus is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year. iOS, Android and web. Best for mood-based recommendations and detailed charts. See alternatives to StoryGraph.

3. Goodreads

The largest reading community, owned by Amazon: friends, reviews, groups and the yearly Reading Challenge. Free, with an account, and ads on the website. iOS, Android and web. Best for following friends and reading other people’s reviews. See alternatives to Goodreads.

4. Hardcover

A modern social tracker with a friends feed, lists, reviews and community-edited book data. Free with an account; the Supporter plan is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year. iOS, Android and web. Best for a Goodreads-style social network with a newer design. See alternatives to Hardcover.

5. Bookmory

A private reading journal with a timer, statistics by author, publisher and tag, and notes from photos. Free with ads; Premium is $3.49 a month or $30.99 a year. iOS, Mac and Android. Best for tracking print books, ebooks and audiobooks in one journal. See alternatives to Bookmory.

When Fable may suit you better

Fable may suit you better if you want to read along with a book club and discuss each chapter, read ebooks in the same app, or use Android: Reliure is a personal tracker designed for print books.

Move from Fable to Reliure

  1. Export your library from Goodreads as a CSV file, if your books are there too, and import it into Reliure.
  2. Otherwise, scan the barcodes of the books you’re reading now, then add tropes and spice as you go.

Reliure exports your library in the Goodreads CSV format, which Goodreads and StoryGraph can both import, so you can always move on. Comparing other apps? See all reading tracker alternatives.

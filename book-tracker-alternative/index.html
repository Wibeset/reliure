Why readers look for a Book Tracker alternative

Book Tracker shares Reliure’s idea of privacy, so the reasons to switch are mostly practical: the 5-book cap of the free version, and a Mac app sold separately.

The best Book Tracker alternatives

We make Reliure, so it comes first. We’ve described every other app as fairly as we can, with what it does best.

1. Reliure

Reliure is as private as Book Tracker, with no account and your library in your own iCloud, but free without a book limit. On top of the reading timer and its Live Activity, you can tag tropes and spice, keep series in order with the next volume ready, spin the wheel to choose your next read, and play along with twelve reading levels, milestones and a monthly challenge.

Price: free, with no ads, no subscription and no account. iPhone, iOS 18 or later. Download on the App Store.

2. Bookly

A reading timer, reading speed, detailed statistics and readathons, with no account needed. The free version is limited and shows ads; Bookly Pro is $29.99 a year or $79.99 for life. iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android. Best for a timer and statistics on every Apple device, if you don’t mind a subscription. See alternatives to Bookly.

3. Bookmory

A private reading journal with a timer, statistics by author, publisher and tag, and notes from photos. Free with ads; Premium is $3.49 a month or $30.99 a year. iOS, Mac and Android. Best for tracking print books, ebooks and audiobooks in one journal. See alternatives to Bookmory.

4. BookBuddy

A catalogue for your home library: barcode scanning, loans, tags and exports to CSV, PDF and HTML. Free up to 50 books; Pro is a one-time $9.99. iPhone, iPad and Mac. Best for cataloguing a large home library and tracking loans. See alternatives to BookBuddy.

5. StoryGraph

Mood, pace and theme tags, detailed charts and recommendations, with audiobooks tracked in minutes. Free with an account; StoryGraph Plus is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year. iOS, Android and web. Best for mood-based recommendations and detailed charts. See alternatives to StoryGraph.

When Book Tracker may suit you better

Book Tracker may suit you better if you want an Apple Watch app, an iPad or Mac app, borrowed-book tracking, Shortcuts, or to log audiobooks in minutes.

Move from Book Tracker to Reliure

  1. Export your Book Tracker library to CSV, or your Goodreads library if your books are there, and import the Goodreads CSV into Reliure.
  2. Otherwise, scan the barcodes of the books you’re reading now: each one takes a few seconds.

Reliure exports your library in the Goodreads CSV format, which Goodreads and StoryGraph can both import, so you can always move on. Comparing other apps? See all reading tracker alternatives.

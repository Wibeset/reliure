Why readers look for a BookBuddy alternative

BookBuddy is built for cataloguing: what you own, where it is and who borrowed it. Readers who want to follow what they’re reading often find it light on journaling, plain in design, and limited at 50 books without Pro.

The best BookBuddy alternatives

We make Reliure, so it comes first. We’ve described every other app as fairly as we can, with what it does best.

1. Reliure

Reliure is built around reading rather than cataloguing. Your current book waits on the home screen with your page in large; every page update becomes a reading session that feeds your statistics, streaks, yearly goal and reading calendar. Add books by scanning their barcode, keep quotes, notes and ratings, and see your shelves in the bookcase, with no book limit and nothing to buy.

Price: free, with no ads, no subscription and no account. iPhone, iOS 18 or later. Download on the App Store.

2. Book Tracker

An indie tracker with no account, a reading timer, an Apple Watch app, and imports from Goodreads, StoryGraph and BookBuddy. Free up to 5 books; Pro is a one-time $11.49. iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac (sold separately). Best for an Apple Watch app and a one-time price. See alternatives to Book Tracker.

3. Bookmory

A private reading journal with a timer, statistics by author, publisher and tag, and notes from photos. Free with ads; Premium is $3.49 a month or $30.99 a year. iOS, Mac and Android. Best for tracking print books, ebooks and audiobooks in one journal. See alternatives to Bookmory.

4. Goodreads

The largest reading community, owned by Amazon: friends, reviews, groups and the yearly Reading Challenge. Free, with an account, and ads on the website. iOS, Android and web. Best for following friends and reading other people’s reviews. See alternatives to Goodreads.

5. StoryGraph

Mood, pace and theme tags, detailed charts and recommendations, with audiobooks tracked in minutes. Free with an account; StoryGraph Plus is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year. iOS, Android and web. Best for mood-based recommendations and detailed charts. See alternatives to StoryGraph.

When BookBuddy may suit you better

BookBuddy may suit you better if you want to catalogue a large home library, track loans, scan with a Bluetooth scanner, or use an iPad or Mac.

Move from BookBuddy to Reliure

  1. Export your BookBuddy library to CSV, or your Goodreads library if your books are there, and import the Goodreads CSV into Reliure.
  2. Otherwise, scan the barcodes of the books you’re reading now: each one takes a few seconds.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my books stored?

On your iPhone and, if you’re signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud, which keeps your library in sync across your devices. Reliure works without an account or a server of its own, so we can’t see your library. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or to a computer, your Reliure data is part of that backup.

How do I back up my library or move to a new iPhone?

If you’re signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account, your library comes back on its own on the new iPhone. To keep a copy of your own:

  1. In Reliure, tap Profile › Data › Export a backup and save the .reliure file to Files or iCloud Drive.
  2. On the new iPhone, open that file, or tap Profile › Data › Restore a backup.
  3. Choose Merge with my library to add what’s missing, or Replace my library to start over from the backup.

The backup includes your books, wishlist, reading sessions, notes, ratings and covers.

Barcode scanning doesn’t work

Reliure needs the camera to read barcodes. Open Settings › Reliure and turn on Camera. Then hold the barcode on the back of the book flat inside the frame, in good light. You can also type the ISBN.

I can’t find my book

Try Advanced search with the title and author, or enter the ISBN. Some books, especially from small publishers, are missing from online catalogues: tap Enter manually to add it yourself.

How do I change a book’s reading dates?

On the book’s page, tap More › Edit, then adjust Started on and Finished on under Reading dates. When you add a book you’ve already read, you can set these dates right away.

How do I export my books to Goodreads or StoryGraph?

Tap Profile › Data › Export as CSV. The file follows Goodreads’ export format, which both Goodreads and StoryGraph can import.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a book from its page (More › Delete), or delete the app to erase all its data from your iPhone. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Is Reliure free?

Yes — no ads and no account. Some bookstore links, such as Amazon, may be affiliate links: we may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you.

How do I add a book?

Scan the ISBN barcode with your camera, search by title or author, or enter the ISBN by hand. Reliure looks up the book’s details in Google Books, Open Library and the catalogue of the Bibliothèque nationale de France.

How do I track my progress?

Enter your current page or a percentage, or adjust it with + and −. Each update is saved as a reading session, which feeds your statistics and reading streaks.

Can I share my reading?

Yes. Share a book, a quote, your bookcase, your reading calendar, a milestone or your year in reading as an image, square for a post or tall for a story, through Instagram, Messages or any app on your iPhone. You can also send your wishlist as a list of gift ideas, with a link to buy each book.

Can I track tropes and spice, like on BookTok?

Yes. Reliure speaks BookTok: tag each book with its tropes, like enemies to lovers or found family, rate its spice from Mild to Scorching, and see your favourite tropes of the year in your statistics. Books from the same series are grouped together, in order, with the next volume to read.

Can Reliure help me choose my next book?

Yes. Spin the wheel and let chance pick among your books to read, or among books from Open Library by the authors you loved and in the genres you read most.

What languages is Reliure available in?

English and French.

Does Reliure work offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection, except looking up a book online by barcode, title or author, and suggestions from Open Library. You can always add a book by hand.

Can I track ebooks or audiobooks?

Reliure is designed for print books, with your progress tracked by page.

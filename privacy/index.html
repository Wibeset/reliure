Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Reliure has no account and no sign-up. Your library, reading sessions, notes and ratings are stored on your iPhone, and we never receive them.

This policy explains what information the Reliure app for iPhone handles and why. Reliure is made by Wibeset, in Canada.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you add to Reliure is stored only on your device:

  • your books and their shelves (to read, reading, read, wishlist);
  • your current page, progress and reading sessions;
  • your notes, quotes and ratings, including ratings by theme;
  • your reading statistics, streaks and reading calendar;
  • your settings, such as the rating themes you chose and your backup reminders.

We have no server that stores this data, and we cannot see it. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or on a computer, this data is included in that backup, under Apple’s terms.

Looking up books

When you scan a barcode, search by title or author, or enter an ISBN, Reliure sends that search directly from your iPhone to Google Books and Open Library to find the book’s details and cover. When they are missing details for an ISBN, Reliure also looks up that ISBN in the catalogue of the Bibliothèque nationale de France (BnF). These services receive the search (for example, the ISBN or the words you typed) and, as with any internet request, your IP address. They do not receive your library, your notes or anything else from the app.

These services handle that information under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy, Internet Archive Terms and Privacy (Open Library is run by the Internet Archive) and the BnF Privacy Policy (in French).

Bookstore links

Reliure can show links to find a book at bookstores such as Amazon, Renaud-Bray, Les libraires or Indigo. When you tap one, the store’s website opens in your browser, and the store handles your visit under its own privacy policy. Each book can also link to its page on Google Books, which Google handles the same way. Some links, such as Amazon’s, include our affiliate ID: if you buy something, we may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you. The store does not send us any information that identifies you.

Backups and sharing

You can export your library to a backup file (.reliure) or to a CSV file, and share a book or your reading calendar as an image. Reliure may remind you to make a backup with a card in the app; it never sends notifications. These happen only when you ask, and the file or content goes only where you choose to save or send it. We never receive it.

Camera and photos

Reliure asks for camera access to read a book’s barcode and to photograph a page when you save a quote. Images are processed on your iPhone: the text of a quote is recognized on the device, only the text you keep is saved, and no image is ever stored or sent anywhere. When you choose a photo from your library instead, Reliure only sees the photo you pick. You can turn off camera access at any time in Settings, and still add books by search or ISBN and type your quotes.

App analytics and crash reports

Reliure does not include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools. If you chose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may share with us anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which help us fix bugs. They do not identify you and do not include your library. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for your name, email address or any account.
  • We don’t show ads or track you across other apps and websites.
  • We don’t sell or share your information.

Deleting your data

Since your data lives on your iPhone, you control it. You can delete books, notes and sessions in the app, and deleting Reliure removes all its data from your device. Backups you made earlier may still contain it until they are replaced or deleted.

Children

Reliure collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Reliure handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.

Contact

Wibeset is responsible for the protection of personal information in Reliure. For any question or request about privacy, write to us at hello@wibeset.ca.

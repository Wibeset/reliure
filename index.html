Features

  • Keep every book

  • Know where you are

    Your page, your progress and the last time you read, always at hand, right down to your Home Screen and Lock Screen with widgets.

Questions

What is Reliure?

Reliure is an iPhone app for tracking the books you read: your library, your current page, your reading sessions, notes, ratings, reading statistics and reading calendar.

Do I need an account?

No. Reliure works without an account or sign-up, and your library is stored on your iPhone.

How do I add a book?

Scan the ISBN barcode with your camera, search by title or author, or enter the ISBN by hand. Reliure looks up the book’s details in Google Books and Open Library.

How do I track my progress?

Enter your current page or a percentage, or adjust it with + and −. Each update is saved as a reading session, which feeds your statistics and reading streaks.

What if I change or lose my iPhone?

Your library is included in your iPhone’s iCloud backups. You can also export a Reliure backup file at any time, with your books, sessions, notes and covers, and restore it on another iPhone. The app gently reminds you when a backup is due, with no notifications.

Can I take my books to another app?

Yes. Reliure exports your library to a CSV file in the Goodreads format, which Goodreads and StoryGraph can import.

Can I share my reading?

Yes. Share a book or your reading calendar as an image, square for a post or tall for a story, through Instagram, Messages or any app on your iPhone.

What languages is Reliure available in?

English and French.

Is Reliure available on Android?

No. Reliure is available for iPhone.

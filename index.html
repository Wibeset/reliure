Questions

What is Reliure?

Reliure is an iPhone app for tracking the books you read: your library, your current page, your reading sessions, notes, ratings and reading statistics.

Do I need an account?

No. Reliure works without an account or sign-up, and your library is stored on your iPhone.

How do I add a book?

Scan the ISBN barcode with your camera, search by title or author, or enter the ISBN by hand. Reliure looks up the book’s details in Google Books and Open Library.

How do I track my progress?

Enter your current page or a percentage, or adjust it with + and −. Each update is saved as a reading session, which feeds your statistics and reading streaks.

Is Reliure available on Android?

No. Reliure is available for iPhone.

Why readers look for a Hardcover alternative

Hardcover is built around a public profile, a friends feed and community book data. Some readers find the app slow, would rather not create another online account, or don’t want statistics such as the full genre breakdown kept behind the Supporter plan.

The best Hardcover alternatives

We make Reliure, so it comes first. We’ve described every other app as fairly as we can, with what it does best.

1. Reliure

Reliure is the quiet counterpart to Hardcover: no account, no feed, and nothing behind a paid plan. Your library stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, the app opens straight on your current book, and everything works offline. Time your sessions with the reading timer, tag tropes and spice, keep series in order, and see your statistics, streaks and yearly goal for free.

Price: free, with no ads, no subscription and no account. iPhone, iOS 18 or later. Download on the App Store.

2. StoryGraph

Mood, pace and theme tags, detailed charts and recommendations, with audiobooks tracked in minutes. Free with an account; StoryGraph Plus is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year. iOS, Android and web. Best for mood-based recommendations and detailed charts. See alternatives to StoryGraph.

3. Book Tracker

An indie tracker with no account, a reading timer, an Apple Watch app, and imports from Goodreads, StoryGraph and BookBuddy. Free up to 5 books; Pro is a one-time $11.49. iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac (sold separately). Best for an Apple Watch app and a one-time price. See alternatives to Book Tracker.

4. Bookly

A reading timer, reading speed, detailed statistics and readathons, with no account needed. The free version is limited and shows ads; Bookly Pro is $29.99 a year or $79.99 for life. iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android. Best for a timer and statistics on every Apple device, if you don’t mind a subscription. See alternatives to Bookly.

5. Goodreads

The largest reading community, owned by Amazon: friends, reviews, groups and the yearly Reading Challenge. Free, with an account, and ads on the website. iOS, Android and web. Best for following friends and reading other people’s reviews. See alternatives to Goodreads.

When Hardcover may suit you better

Hardcover may suit you better if you want a social network for readers, with friends, lists and reviews, if you use Android or the web, or if you track ebooks and audiobooks: Reliure is designed for print books, tracked by page.

Move from Hardcover to Reliure

  1. If you came to Hardcover from Goodreads, export your library from Goodreads as a CSV file (My Books › Import and export) and import it into Reliure.
  2. Otherwise, scan the barcodes of the books you’re reading now: each one takes a few seconds.

Reliure exports your library in the Goodreads CSV format, which Goodreads and StoryGraph can both import, so you can always move on. Comparing other apps? See all reading tracker alternatives.

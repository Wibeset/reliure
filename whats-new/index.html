What’s new in Reliure

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Every version of Reliure, the free reading tracker for iPhone, newest first.

Version 1.8 — November 2026

  • Reading timer. Time your reading sessions on a full-screen timer, with a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Timed sessions add your reading time and pages per hour to your statistics.
  • A new home screen. The book you’re reading fills the screen over its own colours, with your page in large; swipe between the books in progress. Below it: your reading rhythm, your latest quote, what to read next, the month and your bookcase.
  • Your pace. See about how long the rest of a book will take at your own pace.
  • End of a session. When you stop the timer, a wheel of page numbers is already turned to where you most likely stopped: save it in a tap, or add a note or a quote.
  • Finishing a book. A new screen for the last page: what it took, the moment to rate it, and what to read next, the series’ next volume or the wheel.
  • Reading reminder. A notification at the time you choose, only on the days your streak would end.
  • Statistics. A goal ring with a pace mark, and a new streak card.
  • A new look and a new welcome. The style of reliure.me in the app, and a five-step introduction that sets your reading goal for the year.
  • Español. Reliure is now available in Spanish, as well as English and French.

Version 1.7.1 — November 16, 2026

Minor fixes.

Version 1.7 — October 2026

  • Tropes. Tag each book with its tropes, like enemies to lovers or found family, and see your favourite tropes of the year in your statistics.
  • Spice. Rate each book’s spice from Mild to Scorching.
  • Series. Books from the same series are grouped together, in order, with the next volume to read.

Version 1.6 — October 2026

  • The next-read wheel. Let chance pick among your books to read, or among books from Open Library by the authors you loved and in the genres you read most.
  • iCloud sync. Your library on all your devices, through your own iCloud.
  • Wishlist sharing. Send your wishlist as gift ideas, with a link to buy each book.
  • Bookcase. The books of the moment on shelves, ready to share as an image.
  • Author statistics. Pages read by author.

First release — October 20, 2026

Reliure arrives on the App Store, in English and French.
